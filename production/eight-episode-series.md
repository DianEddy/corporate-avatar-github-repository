# Eight-Episode Series

The eight-episode series is a distinct production workstream from the 24-hour Corporate Avatar: Off Duty event broadcast. It follows invited influencers and, where confirmed, celebrities working with thrifters, sewers, upcyclers, designers, tailors, and other makers to develop looks for the event.

## Editorial premise

Each episode documents the people, garments, techniques, transformations, creative decisions, challenges, and stories behind one or more looks. The series is interested in process rather than a simple before-and-after reveal: how identity, craft, memory, labor, and style meet in a garment.

## Proposed episode structure

1. Meet the guest and understand the brief.
2. Meet the maker or makers and select materials, references, and techniques.
3. Develop the look through fittings, tests, alterations, and conversation.
4. Resolve a creative or practical constraint.
5. Capture the final look and the story it carries.
6. Connect the look to the wider 24-hour event.

The structure is a working framework and may adapt to each participant, maker, and production schedule.

Applicants should identify the production role they are seeking and include relevant examples of previous work where available. Makers should describe their techniques, materials, and approach to collaboration. Guests and creators should explain their relationship to clothing, identity, work, or the event's themes.

Indicate whether you are interested in the series, the 24-hour event, or both. Participation, rights, compensation, schedule, and deliverables will be discussed and agreed separately.
