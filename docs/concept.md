# Concept

## The question

Who are you when you are not performing the role your day requires?

## The premise

Corporate Avatar: Off Duty follows one day through the clothes, codes and identities that surround work. From first light to after hours, the experience treats clothing as evidence: of belonging, aspiration, labor, memory, status and refusal.

## The invitation

This is not a conference about fashion from a distance. It is a living exchange between people who make, wear, alter, study, question and move through clothes. The public is invited to listen, participate and bring their own experience into the conversation.

## Editorial principles

- Keep lived experience at the center.
- Make room for contradiction and changing identity.
- Treat clothing as culture, infrastructure and communication.
- Credit contributors and protect private information.
- Clearly label proposals, working material and confirmed details.
