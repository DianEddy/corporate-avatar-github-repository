# Contributing

Corporate Avatar: Off Duty welcomes thoughtful corrections, ideas and collaboration from people working across fashion, culture, production, technology and community.

## Before contributing

- Read [PROJECT-STATUS.md](PROJECT-STATUS.md) so proposals are not mistaken for confirmed details.
- Keep public submissions concise, respectful and relevant to the event.
- Do not include personal phone numbers, private addresses, credentials, unreleased contracts or confidential negotiations.
- Do not submit work you do not have permission to share.

## Ways to contribute

For corrections or public documentation suggestions, open a focused issue or pull request. For participation, partnership, vendor and maker opportunities, use the relevant guide in this repository and contact **partnerships@goodheartapp.com**.

All submissions may be edited for clarity, safety, accuracy and fit with the public project archive.
