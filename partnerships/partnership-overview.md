# Partnership overview

Corporate Avatar: Off Duty is designed for partners who want to support a considered public conversation about clothing, identity, culture and the future of work.

Potential partner categories include fashion and textile, technology, media, hospitality, education, cultural institutions, mobility and community organizations. Opportunities may include founding partnership, program support, in-kind contribution, venue, production, research and public programming.

The public opportunity materials in `public/partnerships/` provide the current overview. For an introductory conversation, contact **edidirect@edimassive.com**. Sponsorship discussions, budgets and agreements remain private and should never be committed to this repository.
