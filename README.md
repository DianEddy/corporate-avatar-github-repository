## Join the team

We are recruiting across five workstreams:

1. **Cast and on-camera contributors** — hosts, correspondents, makers, creators, interviewees, special guests, and participants identified in the Master Script.
2. **Live event production** — producers, stage and set teams, technical crew, venue operations, wardrobe, hospitality, and event-day managers.
3. **Eight-episode series production** — directors, producers, camera and sound teams, editors, stylists, makers, and creator liaison for the series documenting looks made for the event.
4. **Project management and documentation** — coordinators who can keep schedules, decisions, repository content, outreach, and dependencies current.
5. **Sponsorship, partnerships, and budget management** — people who can build relationships, develop proposals, coordinate activations, and track commitments.

Read [Open Roles](cast-and-participation/OPEN-ROLES.md) and the relevant project brief before applying. For cast roles, the Master Script is the authoritative reference for role identifiers and descriptions.

## How to express interest

Email **partnerships@goodheartapp.com** with one of these subject lines:

- `CASTING — [ROLE ID] — [ROLE NAME] — [YOUR NAME]`
- `EVENT PRODUCTION — [ROLE] — [YOUR NAME]`
- `SERIES PRODUCTION — [ROLE] — [YOUR NAME]`
- `PROJECT MANAGEMENT — [ROLE] — [YOUR NAME]`
- `PARTNERSHIPS & BUDGET — [ROLE] — [YOUR NAME]`
- `PARTNERSHIP INQUIRY — [ORGANIZATION] — [YOUR NAME]`

Include your name and location, the exact role or workstream, relevant experience or portfolio links, availability, whether you can work in New York or remotely, and what you can realistically contribute. State whether you are seeking paid, project-based, employment, or voluntary engagement. Do not send passwords, sensitive identity documents, or confidential third-party information.

An expression of interest does not guarantee selection, employment, compensation, or participation. Scope, rights, and terms will be discussed separately.
