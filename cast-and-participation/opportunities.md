# Participation opportunities

We are interested in people with a lived relationship to clothing, work and identity: workers, designers, makers, researchers, performers, organizers, collectors, repairers and curious wearers.

Potential forms of participation include conversation, performance, exhibition, making, documentation, facilitation and production support. Specific calls will be published here when confirmed.

To express interest, email **edidirect@edimassive.com** with a short introduction, your area of interest and links to relevant public work. Please do not send sensitive personal information or materials that are not cleared for review.
