# Public roadmap

This roadmap describes the next public milestones. It is directional, not a production schedule.

1. Establish the creative thesis and public reference materials.
2. Finalize the experience chapters and public program.
3. Confirm venue, production approach and broadcast format.
4. Publish official participation and casting opportunities.
5. Secure and announce aligned partners, sponsors, vendors and makers.
6. Release final attendance information and event communications.
7. Publish approved post-event notes, documentation and outcomes.

Open decisions and changes will be recorded in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md) and [PROJECT-STATUS.md](PROJECT-STATUS.md).
