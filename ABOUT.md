# About Corporate Avatar

Corporate Avatar: Off Duty is a 24-hour live experience about the relationship between clothing, work and identity. It moves through a day in New York, inviting workers, makers, thinkers and wearers to examine the uniforms we inherit and the selves we make visible.

The project is developed by **DMT Outfitters LLC** with **GoodHeart**, a fashion-technology platform exploring better ways to understand, maintain and recirculate what we already own.

This is a public working archive. Confirmed information is separated from proposals in [PROJECT-STATUS.md](PROJECT-STATUS.md). Dates, venue, casting and partner announcements will only be considered official when published here or through the project team.
