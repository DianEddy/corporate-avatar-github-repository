# Public program

Corporate Avatar: Off Duty is structured as four chapters across a 24-hour cycle.

## First light

The day begins with preparation, ritual and the first layer. What do we put on before anyone is watching?

## On the clock

Uniforms, workwear, codes and the clothes that open doors. Who gets to define professionalism, and who gets to rewrite it?

## The in-between

A live exchange on status, belonging and the things we choose to keep. The program opens up space for conversation, interruption and shared reflection.

## After hours

Music, movement, repair and the return to a self that is yours. The day closes without pretending that any identity is singular.

Timing, venue and participation details will be published when confirmed.
